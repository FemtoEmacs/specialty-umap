Insert your own preferences into the AWRS-SMC atlas

Move the sliders below and the trained Transformer (smc-trainer/transformer.lisp) places your preferred specialty on the atlas live — the red star is your placement, and it moves the instant you touch a slider; wherever the sliders sit right now is where your specialty is placed. Each cluster in the legend is labeled with the specialty closest to its center, as a representative example — the cluster is the whole group of similar specialties, not just the one lending its name. Click a cluster's name to jump every slider to that representative specialty's own values. The prediction runs entirely in this page (a JavaScript port of transformer-forward) using the model exactly as trained.
