Insert your own preferences into the AWRS-SMC atlas

Move the sliders below and the trained Transformer (smc-trainer/transformer.lisp) places your preferred specialty on the atlas live — the red star is your placement, and it moves the instant you touch a slider; wherever the sliders sit right now is where your specialty is placed. Cluster names describe the specialties they contain. Click a cluster name to set the sliders to a representative specialty; a blue circle marks that specialty's atlas position so you can compare it with the Transformer's red star. The prediction runs entirely in this page (a JavaScript port of transformer-forward) using the model exactly as trained.
