How this result was calculated

AWRS-SMC used 24 particles over 12 steps to sample nine feature weights (0.5, 1 or 2), neighborhood size (10, 15 or 20), and minimum distance (0.05, 0.1 or 0.3). Every recipe was evaluated with Common Lisp UMAP at seeds 20260905 and 20260917, followed by DBSCAN clustering.

Quality = 0.6 × neighborhood recall + 0.3 × cluster stability across seeds + 0.1 × coverage. Recipes with fewer than two clusters receive zero quality. These are map-structure measures, not candidate-classification accuracy. The small score difference does not establish a robust advantage from two seeds alone.
